Our Story

Founded in 2020, eBooks was born out of a passion for literature and a vision to make reading accessible to everyone, everywhere. We believe that knowledge should know no boundaries.

What started as a small collection of digital books has now grown into a comprehensive platform offering thousands of titles across various genres, from classic literature to contemporary bestsellers.
